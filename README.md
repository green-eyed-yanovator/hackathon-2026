# hackathon-2026


The idea is a community social media map for neighbors and community members

Anyone can create an account and post on a geotag of what's going on

Are you hosting a bbq party?
Looking for local people to hang out with?
Concerned about someone dumping rubbish on your street?
Local gossip?
Trying to see if your neighbours also want to install a bench in the local park?
Did you witness a street fight and want to share it with others?
Want to find where the local amenities are?
